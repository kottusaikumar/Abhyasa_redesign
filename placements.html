Our Placement Support Process

STEP BY STEP TOWARDS YOUR CAREER
  1. 01

    Career Guidance

    Explore relevant opportunities and understand the institute’s placement support.

  2. 02

    Skill Enhancement

    Monthly evaluation tests track student performance and readiness.

  3. 03

    Industry Connect

    Potential employers are connected with students; shortlisting is based on evaluation performance.

  4. 04

    Ongoing Support

    Students may also pursue off-campus drives independently. Hiring depends on the prevailing job scenario.

Regular Evaluation

Evaluation tests are conducted once every month.

Employer Connections

The institute makes efforts to connect potential employers with students.

Student Shortlisting

Students are shortlisted for opportunities based on test performance.

Off-campus Opportunities

Students may also be selected through off-campus drives without institute support.
